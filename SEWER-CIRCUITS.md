# Sewer circuits

Open **Tracks** from the home screen. It contains the original Waterworks freestyle park and two new head-to-head sewer races:

- **Runoff Run:** a 1,360m winding brick interceptor sewer, green water, amber work lights and three maintenance bypasses.
- **The Deep Works:** a 1,740m industrial pressure drain, green lamps, steel ribs, turbines and three longer bypasses.

Both races require every sun ring in order. Shortcuts rejoin before the next checkpoint and shorten the physical route. Narrow bypasses contain debris to jump and whirlpools to avoid. The main route includes closing flood barrels: pump or carry a trick boost through before they shut. Three wipeouts end the player's race. Rivals use the same movement, hazards and earned trick boosts.

## Sewer ladder

Enter **The Sewer Ladder** from Tracks. Beat five rivals across the two circuits:

1. Drain Rat — Runoff Run
2. Valve — The Deep Works
3. Rust — Runoff Run
4. Night Shift — The Deep Works
5. Sludge King — The Deep Works

Two wins unlock the **Drain Crew** outfit; four unlock **Night Shift**. Five unlock the **Sludge Surge** board. Equip these in Select Outfit and Customise Board. Free track races do not advance the ladder. Resetting the ladder retains earned outfits, the board and best times. Existing Hit List ladder and Time Trial progression remain independent.

## Sludge Surge

With the board equipped, press the board-ability key (default **Q**) for five seconds of **+35% base speed** and protection against whirlpools, debris and closing waves. It recharges for fifteen seconds of active play. A mint pulse, faceted shield and orbiting rings show the active ability; completed tricks throw off teal splashes. Failed trick landings still count as wipeouts. Pausing freezes the ability; leaving or finishing a session cancels it.

Admin **Give Me Everything** includes the sewer rewards. **Reset Progress** clears sewer progression alongside other progress; **Full Reset** clears the entire save. Saves stay in the current browser.

For local checks, `race-lab.html` includes both sewer routes, normal-control route following and an optional Sludge Surge preview that never writes ladder progress.
